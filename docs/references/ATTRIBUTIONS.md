## Material Symbols

`_NavigationStateGlyph.js`, `_FacilityGlyph.js`, and `_HazardGlyph.js` embed
selected Material Symbols Rounded SVG paths from Google. The source paths are
Apache-2.0 licensed. LK Robotics UI modifies their placement and scale and
combines them with LK-authored geometry; `ramp`, `handoff`, `dropoff`, and
`collision` are not Material Symbols paths.
